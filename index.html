Roboquest — Weapon & Affix Stat Browser

A sortable, filterable reference for every weapon in Roboquest — base damage, fire rate, range, crit multiplier, ammo/energy cost, affixes, altfire and reload. This is a browser, not a verdict: it hands you the sourced numbers side by side and leaves the pick to you. Nothing here is saved or sent anywhere — it renders from the data in this page in your browser.

Click a column header to sort. Filter by category or search by name. A "DPS est." column multiplies base damage × fire rate as a straight, labeled estimate — it is not a game-verified number, and it is left blank wherever either source field is unresolved rather than computed from a guess.
